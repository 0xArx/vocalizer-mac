Ready in a minute.

Requires a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 14.2 or later.

  1. Download

    Download the zip and open it. Drag Vocalizer to your Applications folder.

  2. Open it

    Vocalizer lives in the menu bar. For video sync, click Install in its menu and enter your password once.

  3. Switch it on

    Flip the switch, then click Allow when macOS asks about System Audio Recording. Play something.

Good to know.

Is it free?

Yes. Vocalizer is free and open source under the MIT license, and the source code is public.

Does it send my audio anywhere?

No. Separation runs entirely on your Mac. Vocalizer only goes online to check once a day for a new version, and to download the Best model if you choose it. See the privacy policy.

Why does macOS ask for System Audio Recording?

Vocalizer needs to hear what your Mac plays in order to remove the music. macOS asks once. Nothing is recorded or saved.

Why is there a delay?

The model needs a short slice of sound to tell voices from music. With video sync on, video players wait for the voices, so you won't notice it in videos. Games and calls do hear it; use Speech mode for calls.

Does it remove every instrument perfectly?

It removes most music very well. Heavy reverb, backing vocals and choirs may leave traces. Try Clean or Best for the cleanest result.

Can I install it from the Terminal?

Yes. Paste this into Terminal: it downloads the latest version, installs the app and the video sync device (macOS asks for your password once), and opens Vocalizer.

$ curl -fsSL https://vocalizer-mac.vercel.app/install.sh | bash
Does it work on Intel Macs?

No. Vocalizer needs Apple silicon (M1 or later), because it runs its models on the Neural Engine and GPU.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit Vocalizer from its menu and drag it to the Trash. To also remove the video sync device, run curl -fsSL https://vocalizer-mac.vercel.app/uninstall.sh | bash.
