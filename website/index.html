How it works

One switch in your menu bar.

Turn it on and every app plays without its music: YouTube in any browser, Apple Music, Spotify, podcasts, lectures, films. Speech, singing and dialogue come through clearly.

Turn it off and your Mac sounds exactly as before. Press ⌥ ⇧ V to switch it from anywhere.

Works with every app

Vocalizer works on your Mac's sound itself, so there's nothing to install in each app or browser.

Lips stay in sync

Video players hold the picture back by exactly the processing delay, so faces and voices line up.

Private by design

Audio is processed on your Mac's Neural Engine and GPU. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Mute swear words

An optional switch silences swear words as they play, recognized on your Mac, with no extra delay. Needs macOS 26 or later.

Speakers and headphones

Plug in headphones or connect AirPods and the sound follows. Your volume keys keep working.

Signed and notarized

Signed with an Apple Developer ID and checked by Apple, so it opens without warnings. Free and open source.

Install

Ready in a minute.

Requires a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 14.2 or later.

Download

Download the zip and open it. Drag Vocalizer to your Applications folder.

Open it

Vocalizer lives in the menu bar as a microphone icon. For video sync, click Install in its menu and enter your password once.

Switch it on

Flip the switch, then click Allow when macOS asks about System Audio Recording. Play something.

$ curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/0xArx/vocalizer-mac/main/install.sh | bash

Prefer the Terminal? This installs the app and the video sync device in one go. To remove it later, run the same command with uninstall.sh.

Questions

Good to know.

Is it free?

Yes. Vocalizer is free and open source under the MIT license. The source code is on GitHub.

Does it send my audio anywhere?

No. Separation runs entirely on your Mac. Vocalizer only connects to GitHub to check once a day for a new version, and to download the Best model if you choose it. See the privacy policy.

Why does macOS ask for System Audio Recording?

Vocalizer needs to hear what your Mac plays in order to remove the music. macOS asks once. Nothing is recorded or saved.

Why is there a delay?

The model needs a short slice of sound to tell voices from music. With video sync on, video players wait for the voices, so you won't notice it in videos. Games and calls do hear it; use Speech mode for calls.

Does it remove every instrument perfectly?

It removes most music very well. Heavy reverb, backing vocals and choirs may leave traces. Try Clean or Best for the cleanest result.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

No. Vocalizer needs Apple silicon (M1 or later), because it runs its models on the Neural Engine and GPU.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit Vocalizer from its menu and drag it to the Trash. To also remove the video sync device, run the uninstall command from the GitHub page.
