Private by design. Not by promise.

An app that hears everything your Mac plays has to earn your trust. Vocalizer is built so it doesn't have to ask for it: you can check every part of this yourself.

  • Fully on your Mac

    The music is removed by your Mac's own Neural Engine and GPU. No servers, no cloud, works offline.

  • Nothing recorded, nothing sent

    Sound is processed in memory and played straight back. It is never saved to disk and never uploaded.

  • No account, no tracking

    No sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no cookies. Vocalizer doesn't know who you are and doesn't want to.

  • Open source

    All of the code is public, so anyone can check exactly what it does. Only the maintainer can publish releases.

  • Signed and checked by Apple

    Every release is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so macOS opens it without warnings.

  • Updates ask first

    New versions only install when you say so, and only after their signature is verified. Its one connection is checking for updates.

Mute swear words, too.

Turn on Mute swear words and Vocalizer silences them as they play, recognized by your Mac's own speech recognition, with no extra delay. Good for family viewing, and for keeping your ears clean.

Works best in Best and Clean modes, and needs macOS 26 or later. Like any speech recognition, it can occasionally miss a word.

Ready in a minute.

Requires a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 14.2 or later.

  1. Download

    Download Vocalizer and drag it to your Applications folder. It's free, with nothing to buy inside.

  2. Open it

    It lives in the menu bar. For video, click Install in its menu once, so lips stay in sync with the voice.

  3. Switch it on

    Flip the switch and click Allow when macOS asks about System Audio Recording. That's it.

Questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. No price, no ads, no subscription and nothing to buy inside. It's open source under the MIT license.

Does it change Qur'an recitation?

No. Recitation is voice, and Vocalizer keeps voices. It only removes what isn't voice, so recitation and adhan come through as they are.

Will it catch every bit of music?

Most music is removed very well, especially in Clean and Best modes. Heavy reverb, choirs and backing vocals can leave faint traces. If something matters a lot to you, use Best mode.

Does it work on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok?

Yes, in any browser and in any Mac app, because it works on your Mac's sound itself rather than inside one app.

Can anyone see what I watch or listen to?

No. Vocalizer has no account and sends nothing anywhere. The sound is processed on your Mac and never saved. Its only connection is checking for updates. See the privacy policy.

Is there a delay?

A short one (about 0.4 s in the default Fast mode). With video sync, video players wait for the voice, so in videos you won't notice it. Use Calls mode for calls and games.

Does it work on iPhone or Windows?

Not yet. Vocalizer is for Macs with Apple silicon.
